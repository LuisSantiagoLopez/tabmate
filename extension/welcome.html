Tabmate

You work once.
Tabmate does it forever.

Tabmate is now watching. It learns the tasks you repeat in the browser and then does them for you. Here is how to see it in under a minute.

  1. Do a task once

    In the sandbox CRM: pick a status, click Export, choose a format, name the file, click Create export.

  2. Do it again

    Same steps. Halfway through, the pill at the bottom says Learned.

  3. Start it a third time

    Pick a status and click Export. Tabmate takes over and finishes it. Press Esc any time to stop.

Open the sandbox Pin Tabmate from the puzzle icon in the toolbar to see what it learned.

Stays on your computer

Everything is saved in your browser. Tabmate makes no network calls.

Never touches secrets

Passwords, card numbers, CVV and SSN fields are never recorded. Tabmate stops and asks you to fill them.

Waits on big buttons

Pay, buy, delete or transfer? Tabmate stops and lets you click it.
